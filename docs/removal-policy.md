# Removal Policy

**Status:** draft; a private contact will be published at launch.

A request is reviewed by the owner, who may correct, hide, quarantine or
remove a route; nothing is removed automatically. A source that is no longer
available does not by itself remove a route.

## How to ask

- Use the route's **Report a problem** form and choose **Author removal
  request** or **Rights-holder request**, or
- open an issue in the [BikeMapy issue tracker](https://github.com/diamond447/BikeMapy/issues)
  and ask for a private channel in the first message.

Do not publish identity documents, addresses or other sensitive data.

## What to include

- the BikeMapy route URL or ID;
- the BikeForum post URL and, if relevant, the Mapy.com URL;
- your role (author, rights holder, representative, or the person concerned);
- what you want done: correct or remove attribution, remove the GPX file, or
  hide the route;
- a short reason, and a reply address if you want an answer.

The owner may ask for reasonable verification of a request made on behalf of
someone else.

## Outcome

The owner records the decision and its reason. A removal deletes the GPX file
and keeps the source URL and decision so the route is not imported again.
Cached page HTML is cleared within 24 hours anyway; copies in backups expire
with the backups (30 daily snapshots on the host, 90 offline) unless they are
removed earlier for an urgent request. A report's contact e-mail is deleted
90 days after it is closed.
